About the shop

The Sandwich Shop is a small, family-owned and operated restaurant in Modesto, serving lunch and dinner with catering, party trays and decorations for special occasions. Sandwiches come on a choice of breads with a choice of cheeses, and every sandwich includes mayo, mustard, lettuce, tomato and onion.
