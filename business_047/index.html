Hard Times Cafe is a worker-owned, all-day vegetarian and vegan cafe in Cedar-Riverside, open late every day. It serves breakfast all day, salads, sandwiches and platters, with takeout and dine-in, and is listed as cash only.

A late-night community cafe where a real website could settle hours, the cash-only note and the all-day breakfast menu.
