Pho, boba and taiyaki — all under one roof.

Taki Taco Pho Restaurant & Bakery is a family-owned Vietnamese restaurant and bakery on North Dirksen Parkway: Saigon-style beef pho in generous portions, plus sugarcane juice, sweet boba teas and fish-shaped taiyaki waffles.

A restaurant and a bakery in one.

Run by a local husband-and-wife team, the Dirksen Parkway location pairs a full Vietnamese menu with the bakery treats the original Taki Taco shop is known for — rolled gelato tacos, taiyaki and sweet drinks. Visitors note the large portions and the vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free choices.
