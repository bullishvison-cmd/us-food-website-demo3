Bold barbecue, cooked low and slow on Jewella Avenue.

Louisiana Smokehouse is a Black-owned barbecue truck serving generous plates, salads and sandwiches from its spot on Jewella Avenue, with outdoor seating, curbside pickup and takeout.

About the business

The truck keeps its menu focused on smoke and Southern sides: barbecue plates built around brisket and ribs, plus sandwiches and salads for a quicker stop. Portions are described by visitors as generous, and the brisket quesadillas get named again and again.
