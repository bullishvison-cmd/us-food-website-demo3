About the shop

Don Rafas Taco Shop has served Stockton since 2017. The shop is family-owned, offers takeout and curbside pickup, and is known for its salsa bar, where diners choose their own sauces and sides. Parking is available on site.

From the counter

Tacos

Asada, pastor and chicken, with sour cream, tomato, cooked onion, avocado, white cheese and jalapeño.

Quesadilla Super

Beef, cheese, pico de gallo, lettuce, avocado, jalapeño and sour cream.

Burritos

Regular and super burritos, plus asada fries.

Tortas and nachos

Tortas, nachos and super nachos for sharing.

Menudo and birria

Visitor favourites mentioned across listings, alongside fresh agua fresca.
