One kitchen, three traditions: Lao, Thai & Vietnamese.

Rock Pho'd is a husband-and-wife Asian fusion restaurant on East Riverside Boulevard — pho and banh mi beside spicy Thai curries, Lao sausage and Chinese stir-fries, every dish cooked fresh to order.

Call (779) 774-4146 Email the restaurant Get directions

Cooked fresh, by the owners, to order.

Rock Pho'd was built by husband-and-wife team Souky and Bonnie as a neighbourhood restaurant with a wide menu: Vietnamese favourites like pho and banh mi, Thai curries and noodle dishes, Lao plates and Chinese stir-fries, with vegetarian-friendly choices among them.

Visitors praise the generous portions, fast service and the signature rice paper rolls and fragrant pho.
