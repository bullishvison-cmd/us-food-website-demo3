Tres leches, baked fresh, the way Rockford loves it.

Las Canelitas Bakery is a Mexican bakery on Broadway in Rockford, known for its tres leches cakes, fresh baked goods and a small grocery section stocked with imported Mexican products.

A neighbourhood Mexican bakery.

Visitors praise the tres leches cakes and the cheese empanada, and the shop pairs its bakery counter with a small grocery section of imported Mexican products — a practical stop for both a celebration cake and pantry staples.
