About Darbar Indian Restaurant

Darbar's menu centres on traditional specialties: tandoor cooking, rich curries and vegetarian dishes, with a daily lunch buffet for a wider taste in one sitting.

The restaurant also offers takeout and delivery through third-party ordering pages. Visitors return for the grilled platters and the long-running favourites below, from Chicken Tikka Masala to Tandoori Chicken. The split lunch-and-dinner schedule means the kitchen closes between seatings on most days, so plan around the afternoon break.
