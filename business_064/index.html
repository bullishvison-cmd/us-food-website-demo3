Darbar is a family-owned halal Indian restaurant in Baltimore's Fells Point area, serving traditional specialties alongside a daily lunch buffet.

Call (410) 563-8008 Email the restaurant Get directions

A neighbourhood table for Indian classics.

Darbar's menu centres on traditional specialties: tandoor cooking, rich curries and vegetarian dishes, with a daily lunch buffet for a wider taste in one sitting. The restaurant also offers takeout and delivery through third-party ordering pages.

Visitors return for the grilled platters and the long-running favourites below, from Chicken Tikka Masala to Tandoori Chicken. The split lunch-and-dinner schedule means the kitchen closes between seatings on most days, so plan around the afternoon break.
