A bakery with deep roots.

Alkazar is a long-standing Elizabeth bakery and pizzeria counter, known for its warm, familiar bakery case and for South American flavours that regulars have returned to for decades.

The counter covers the bakery basics done well: pastries, breads and cakes, with an Uruguayan and Argentine character that sets it apart from a standard bake shop.
