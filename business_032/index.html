Custom, from first sketch to last slice.

Todo Postres is a custom-cake bakery: celebration cakes are made to order for weddings, quinceañeras and birthdays, and the counter also carries everyday treats for walk-in visitors.

The shop sits on West Oklahoma Avenue in Milwaukee and has served the neighbourhood since 2014.
