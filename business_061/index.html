Norfolk, Virginia · Since 1988

Small-batch baking, early mornings, sold out by afternoon.

Naas Bakery is a family-owned Norfolk bakery that has been baking since 1988. Pastries, bread, doughnuts, cookies, cupcakes and custom cakes, made fresh and sold until the case is empty.

Call (757) 623-3858 Email the bakery Get directions

Baked in the morning, gone by mid-afternoon.

Naas Bakery opens at 6am, Tuesday through Saturday, and closes at 3pm or when the day's baking sells out. The shop is closed on Sundays and Mondays, so the weekend window is Saturday morning.

Visitors come back for the classics: glazed doughnuts, butter twists and cinnamon rolls, plus apple fritters, pineapple upside-down cake and cheese rolls. Custom cakes are available for special occasions.

Ordering is handled through a third-party ordering page linked from directory listings; the bakery does not run its own website.
