Small shop. Fresh donuts. Mornings only.

My Sister's Lil Donut Shoppe has been frying up cake donuts, raised rings, long johns, cinnamon rolls and twists in Oswego since 2012 — and it closes at noon, so early birds get the pick of the case.

Come early — the case empties fast.

With a 6:30am open and a noon close, this is a morning shop through and through. The menu sticks to donut-shop classics done well: cake donuts, raised rings, long johns, cinnamon rolls and twists.

Find it on South Madison Street in downtown Oswego.
