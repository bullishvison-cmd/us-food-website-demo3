About

Come early — the case empties fast

With a 6:30am open and a noon close, this is a morning shop through and through. The menu sticks to donut-shop classics done well: cake donuts, raised rings, long johns, cinnamon rolls and twists. Find it on South Madison Street in downtown Oswego.
