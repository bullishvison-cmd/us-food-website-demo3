About

About the truck

El Boulevard Fritura y Chimichurri is a late-night Dominican fritura truck in Paterson, serving chimichurri sandwiches and fritura into the early hours.

The counter covers chicharrón, chuleta, longaniza, tostones and jugo de chinola. Hours run late — please call ahead to confirm tonight's spot.
