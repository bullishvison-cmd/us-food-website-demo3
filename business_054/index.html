A neighbourhood Mexican table

Mexico Bar & Grill pairs a full Mexican menu with a bar, so the same room works for a family dinner and for drinks with friends later in the evening.

The restaurant sits on McBride Avenue in Paterson and keeps weekend hours until 2am, making it one of the later options in the neighbourhood.
