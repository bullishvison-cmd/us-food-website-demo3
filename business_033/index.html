A newer kitchen with a classic menu.

Open since 2023, Vanakkam India covers the Indian restaurant staples Rockford visitors look for: rich curries, tandoori cooking, lentil soups and fresh naan, with vegetarian dishes like Palak Paneer and Chana Masala alongside meat curries.
