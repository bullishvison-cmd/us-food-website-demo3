About the business

The restaurant leans on freshly prepared dishes and group dining: family meals and combo platters are promoted through its social posts, alongside traditional aguas frescas and sodas. Grilled chicken, beef and shrimp feature across the menu in street-style and Tex-Mex seasoning.
