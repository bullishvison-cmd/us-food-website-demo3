About the shop

Tonantzin has served Stockton since 2018. The restaurant is women-owned and offers takeout in a casual setting, with private parking on site. Its menu leans on tortas, tacos and quesadillas made with homemade birria.

From the counter

Keto Tacos

Tacos built on a burnt cheese tortilla crust.

Quesadilla Tacos

Grilled quesatacos loaded with cheese and homemade birria, served with a rich consommé for dipping.

Tortas

A Mexican sandwich on a soft, toasted roll with mayo, lettuce, tomato, onion, cilantro and cheese.
