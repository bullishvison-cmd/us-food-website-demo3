Banana Cherry Bakery is a Rockford bakery and cafe known for its banana bread, brioche and pan au lait — plus pies, cookies, rolls, meat pies, scones, cakes and Italian coffee.

Baked slow, served warm.

Banana Cherry Bakery has been baking in Rockford since 2008. The counter leans European — brioche, pan au lait and scones sit alongside banana bread, pies and cookies — and Italian coffee rounds out a cafe visit.

It is a small, independent bakery on South Alpine Road, and the kind of place regulars visit for one favourite loaf and leave with something extra.
