North and South Indian cooking, plus a lunch buffet.

Gateway to India has served Springfield since 2011 — tandoori dishes, curries and fresh naan, a popular lunch buffet, and a broad vegetarian and vegan selection.

A local favourite for over a decade.

Gateway to India is known locally for its approachable, home-style dishes and generous portions — the lunch buffet lets first-time visitors sample widely before ordering à la carte, and staff are noted for pointing out vegan and vegetarian options.
