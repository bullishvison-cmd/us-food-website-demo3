About Aztecas's

The truck's focus is tacos in all their forms: chicken, steak, al pastor, barbacoa and birria, plus heavily stuffed quesadillas with melted cheese, guacamole, radishes, jalapeños, cilantro and grilled onions.

The asada tacos are the signature order.
